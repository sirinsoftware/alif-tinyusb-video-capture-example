original work of this project, and (with the exception noted below) are
byte-identical to the pack baselines stored alongside them as
"<name>.base@<version>" files.

  AlifSemiconductor::Ensemble 2.0.2

      Agreement, see LICENSE-ALIF.txt.

      https://github.com/ARM-software/CMSIS_6
      Licence: Apache-2.0.

      carries an Arm Limited copyright notice and an Apache-2.0
      SPDX identifier; the licence text is available at
      https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

  ARM::CMSIS-FreeRTOS 11.2.0
      https://github.com/ARM-software/CMSIS-FreeRTOS

      configuration file. Its header identifies FreeRTOS Kernel V10.4.0,
